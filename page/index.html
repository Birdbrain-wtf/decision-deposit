Kusama governance

Collaborative decision deposits

A referendum on Kusama cannot be voted on until someone places its decision deposit. This pool lets several groups put in what they can. The payment that fills it places the deposit, and when the referendum ends every group is paid back to the address it paid from.

This contract has been tested on a copy of the live chain. Nobody outside Birdbrain has reviewed it yet. Do not put in KSM you cannot afford to have locked for the length of a referendum.

    The problem

    One account has to carry the whole deposit.

    Kusama's governance takes a decision deposit from a single account. On the Wish for Change track it is 666.67 KSM. The deposit comes back when the referendum ends, so it costs nothing in the end, but few groups can lock that much up for weeks. A referendum that nobody can carry on their own times out without a vote, however many people would have backed it.

    Sharing the deposit has been asked for since 2023 and has not been built yet:

    • August 2023. Moonbeam's team asked the forum about collaborative decision deposits. One reply suggested that, on a chain with smart contracts, a contract holding its own account is the natural place for it. That is what this pool is.
    • February 2024. A proposal for a referendum track that places decision deposits, written up as Fellowship RFC 73. It was closed without being adopted.
    • 2025. RFC 151, crowdsourced decision deposits, would have built sharing into governance itself. It came up again in the OpenGov Adjustments 2025 thread. The RFC was closed, and the issue to add it to the code is still open.

    This pool needs no change to governance. It works on Kusama Asset Hub as it runs today.

    How it works

    Five steps, and none of them needs us.

    01A pool is fixed to one referendum and its exact deposit when it is created. It has no owner and cannot be changed.
    02Groups pay KSM in. Each payment is recorded against the address it came from, and can be taken back while the pool is open.
    03The payment that fills the pool places the deposit on the referendum, from the pool's own account. If that attempt fails, anyone can place it later.
    04When the referendum ends, anyone presses one button to bring the deposit back. A watcher does it in the next block.
    05Every group is paid back to the address it paid from. If the pool never fills, the same happens after its deadline.
    Before you pay in

    What this does not do.

    A killed referendum loses the deposit

    Approved, rejected, cancelled and timed out all refund. Killed does not, and the pool cannot recover it. Kills are rare and are meant for proposals that abuse the system.

    Nothing fires by itself

    A referendum ending writes a record on the chain, and a record cannot call a contract. Someone sends the transaction that brings the deposit back. Anyone can, including you, from this page.

    It has not been reviewed

    The contract is about 230 lines and holds other people's KSM. It passed 88 checks on a copy of Kusama Asset Hub, and on Paseo, Polkadot's test network, it placed a real decision deposit. An outside review is still to come.

    One pool, one referendum

    A pool cannot be moved to another referendum. If the referendum it was made for times out before the pool fills, everyone is repaid after the deadline.

    Paying from a Polkadot account or a multisig

    A group without an Ethereum-style wallet can pay in from any Kusama Asset Hub account, including a multisig, with a revive.call extrinsic in polkadot.js. Set dest to the pool contract above, value to the amount in planck (1 KSM is 1,000,000,000,000), and data to 0xd7bb99ba. The payment is recorded against that account and repaid to it. To take it back while the pool is open, send the same call with value 0 and data 0x3ccfd60b.
